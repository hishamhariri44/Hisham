About

Curious by nature.
Filmmaker by choice.

I’m a filmmaker, video editor and colourist with over eight years of experience across commercial work, documentary stories and photography.

I’ve documented Al-Ihsan’s field work in rural Aleppo, filmed architecture and cultural events, and created campaigns for products, spaces and businesses. I work from the first idea through filming, editing and colour, bringing AI into the process when a project calls for an imagined world.

What connects it all is attention: to people, to light, and to the feeling a frame leaves behind.
